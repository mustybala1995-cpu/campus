Quick Tool: Free Time Estimator

Plan your week around classes and events

How it works

Estimate your weekly free time

The calculator starts with the 168 hours in a week. It subtracts your weekly class hours and assumes 8 hours of sleep each night, which is 56 hours per week.

Free time = 168 − class hours − 56 sleep hours

Example: 4 class hours × 5 days gives 20 class hours. Your estimated free time is 168 − 20 − 56 = 92 hours.

This estimate includes studying, travelling, meals, recreation and other activities.

Enter your numbers and press calculate.
